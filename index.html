Academic Biography

Morris Bai is a master's student in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) at Rochester Institute of Technology in Rochester, New York, USA. He earned a Master of Fine Arts from National Taiwan Normal University and a Bachelor of Fine Arts from National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan. His interdisciplinary background in fine arts, visual communication, and interaction design informs his research on the intersection of human experience and emerging technologies.

His research focuses on human-artificial intelligence (AI) interaction, cognitive workload, interaction cost, and user-centered AI interface design. His current work investigates how different AI interface designs influence cognitive effort, interaction efficiency, task performance, and user experience during information-seeking tasks. Drawing on theories and methods from HCI, cognitive psychology, and design research, he aims to contribute both theoretical insights and practical design guidelines for developing intuitive, trustworthy, and human-centered AI systems.

Morris aspires to combine artistic creativity with human-centered research to design meaningful digital experiences that address real-world needs. He believes that his background in art, programming, and design provides a distinctive perspective for solving complex User Experience challenges and advancing the design of future interactive technologies.

Rhetorical Analysis

In this rhetorical analysis assignment, I examined an infographic by applying the seven elements of the rhetorical situation: writer, audience, purpose, exigence, subject, context, and genre. The goal was to explain how these rhetorical elements work together to communicate the infographic's message effectively. In addition to analyzing the visual design, I also incorporated academic sources to support my interpretation and followed APA style for citations and references. Completing this assignment required me to connect rhetorical theory with a real-world example while organizing the analysis into a clear academic structure.
This assignment taught me that successful rhetorical analysis depends on accurately understanding both rhetorical concepts and the source being analyzed. I learned to distinguish between different rhetorical elements, such as rhetorical situation and rhetorical appeals, and to explain each element according to its specific role in communication. I also realized the importance of interpreting visual genres correctly instead of making assumptions about their purpose. Furthermore, I strengthened my understanding of academic writing by recognizing the need for precise quotations, proper attribution of factual information, consistent APA formatting, and careful proofreading to ensure that every paragraph remains focused, logically connected, and fully supported by evidence.

Annotated Bibliography

This assignment focused on developing a research problem statement and creating an annotated bibliography to support my research proposal. I wrote a concise problem statement by identifying the research context, the specific problem, and its significance, and then evaluated academic sources using the provided rubric before writing annotations that included a summary, critical evaluation, and reflection. In addition to organizing the content according to APA guidelines, I also practiced using in-text citations and compiling a reference page to ensure that my sources were presented in a consistent academic format.
Through this assignment, I learned that writing an annotated bibliography involves more than summarizing articles; it requires evaluating the quality and relevance of each source and explaining how it contributes to my own research. I also became more aware of the importance of integrating citations naturally into my writing instead of placing them mechanically at the beginning or end of paragraphs. More importantly, I realized that academic writing is built on the accumulation of regular reading. Continuously engaging with scholarly literature not only provides the theoretical foundation for future research but also stimulates new ideas and helps identify meaningful research gaps. Rather than searching for sources only when writing an assignment, developing a habit of reading academic articles allows me to build a stronger knowledge base that supports critical thinking and the development of well-informed research questions. Furthermore, I strengthened my understanding of APA style by paying closer attention to details such as reference completeness, formatting consistency, and indentation.

Mock IRB

This assignment required me to complete a mock Institutional Review Board (IRB) application for my proposed HCI research on balancing cognitive load and interaction cost in AI interfaces. The IRB form covered every stage of the research process, including the research problem, research questions, expected benefits, participant recruitment, ethical considerations, data collection procedures, risk assessment, informed consent, and supporting research materials. Completing the form required me to think beyond the research idea itself and consider how the study would be conducted responsibly while protecting participants' rights, privacy, and well-being throughout the research process.
This assignment taught me that conducting research involves much more than designing an experiment. Before collecting any data, researchers must carefully justify every aspect of the study, from participant selection and recruitment methods to confidentiality, risk management, and informed consent. I also realized that ethical considerations should be incorporated into the research design from the beginning rather than treated as a final requirement. Thinking through these issues helped me better understand how ethical research practices improve the credibility and quality of a study. In addition, completing the IRB application allowed me to examine my research proposal from multiple perspectives, making my study design more systematic, practical, and participant-centered while preparing me for future academic research.

Research Proposal

This assignment required me to develop a complete research proposal based on my previous work, including the problem statement, annotated bibliography, and IRB application. The proposal integrated all major components of an academic research project, including the introduction, literature review, research questions, research context, methodology, data collection and analysis, and the significance of the study. I also applied APA formatting, synthesized evidence from multiple scholarly sources, and included supporting materials such as the informed consent form, SUS, NASA-TLX questionnaires, and interview protocol in the appendices. Writing the proposal allowed me to transform an initial research idea into a structured and feasible research plan.
Through this assignment, I learned that every section of a research proposal must support the others rather than being written independently. While writing my proposal, I realized that the research questions became the foundation for selecting an appropriate methodology, which then determined the data collection instruments and analysis methods. This process taught me to constantly check whether each part of the proposal aligned with my research objectives instead of treating each section as a separate writing task. I also learned that a well-designed proposal requires logical consistency from beginning to end, ensuring that the identified research gap, proposed methods, and expected contributions all address the same research problem. This experience improved my ability to think systematically about research design and prepared me to conduct a study that is both coherent and feasible.
